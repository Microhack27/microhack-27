Our Centre

MICROCHIP – Centre for Edge and Embedded Intelligence

The MICROCHIP – Centre for Edge and Embedded Intelligence is located at Easwari Engineering College, Chennai. It was inaugurated on 5 September 2026, marking an important milestone in promoting innovation, hands-on learning, embedded technologies and edge intelligence.

The centre gives students opportunities to explore emerging technologies, conduct research and develop innovative real-world projects. As part of its commitment to innovation and technical excellence, the centre is proud to organise MICROHACK'27, a National Level 36 Hours Hackathon.

Guidelines

Eligibility & Rules

Everything you need to know before you register.

01 Who can participate

  • Undergraduate or postgraduate students in EEE, ECE, CSE, CSD, Embedded Systems, Mechatronics, Instrumentation or related disciplines.
  • Professional engineers and developers working in semiconductor design, IoT, industrial automation, medical devices, automotive electronics or embedded firmware.
  • Research scholars, PhD candidates and faculty working in embedded systems, microcontroller architectures or low-power electronics.
  • Independent developers and hobbyists with demonstrable hands-on embedded systems experience.

02 Technical prerequisites

  • Microcontrollers: peripherals such as GPIO, UART, SPI, I²C, ADC, DAC, DMA, timers and PWM.
  • Firmware: proficiency in C or C++ for embedded targets; FreeRTOS is an advantage.
  • Debugging: breakpoints, serial logging, and oscilloscopes or logic analysers where needed.
  • Connectivity: BLE, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, CAN, RS-485, UART, SPI or I²C, depending on your problem statement.
  • Preferred, not mandatory: experience with MPLAB X IDE, MCC, XC compilers and Harmony v3.

03 General rules

  • Free registration. There is no registration fee.
  • Team size: 1 to 4 members. Choose your preferred problem statement while registering.
  • Bring your own sensors, controllers and peripheral components. Microchip kits are allocated by problem statement and availability.
  • Set up one day before: install the software, compile a sample project and verify that your kit can be programmed.
  • Original work only. Plagiarised or pre-built solutions will be disqualified.
  • Build within the 36 hours. Work must be developed during the event window.
  • Carry a valid college or organisation ID card and your laptop with charger.
  • Respect everyone. Follow the code of conduct, venue rules and the organisers' instructions.
  • Fair play: the decisions of the organisers and judges are final.
  • Judging covers innovation, technical depth, working prototype, use of Microchip platforms and presentation.
